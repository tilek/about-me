# My Birth and Early Childhood

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

After getting married in 1980 my parents struggled to have their first child for  
several years. My understanding is that it was likely because my mother was too  
stressed living with her mother-in-law at her house.  

To address the issue my dad hustled to get a studio apartment from Soviet  
government. They moved and soon after I was born on June 9, 1985.  

To signify that I was long-awaited child they long dreamed about my dad  
suggested calling me "Tilek" as in Kyrgyz it means "a wish" or "a dream".  

It fills me at a very deep level to know I was wanted and waited for so much!  

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I was born in in modern-day Bishkek, the capital of Kyrgyzstan.  
Back then and until 1991 it was all still a part of Soviet Union and the city  
used to be called Frunze after one of the key leaders during Lenin's revolution.  

I was the first out of two children in a full family with both of my parents.  
For 4.5 years, I was the only child and had a lot of attention from my mother,  
who was a stay-at-home mom at that time.  

My grandma helped my parents care for me as well during the first 5 years or so  
until my little brother Meder and my little cousin Aidar were both born in 1990.  
She then switched to help her other daughter care for Aidar.  

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Actual birth turned out to be difficult for my mom. I was upside down in her  
belly and doctor recommended her to do a Ceasar's section. She was under  
anesthesia but she was told the birth was very bloody and she had multiple  
long-term side effects after.  

I was told that I often got sick and my parents spent a lot of time bringing me  
to doctors and curing my diseases.  

One thing I found out only in 2025, when I was 40, is that immediately after  
birth I was removed into a special room and felt my mother's warmth only several  
days later.  

It's hard to know for sure but I feel it could have been the basis of the  
anxiety deep in my subconscious and anxious attachment I constantly feel  
but hopefully some day will cure.  

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Around that time I already felt special and admired. As long as I remember her,  
my grandm loved retelling the story how I already learned to read when I was 4.  
She and my parents told me that whenever they took a bus, I loved reading aloud  
all the signs I could see. Based on such wealth compliments from my grandmother  
I already felt I was a genius and I was special.  

I am also infinitely grateful to her for feeling so much love, care, attention,  
and admiration from her side. It definitely was a huge part of my self-belief  
and ambition during my whole life after.
