Who is this document for:
- It's for people who I am starting to collaborate with informally before written agreement.

If you are starting to work with me without a contract, let's agree to the following more informally:

Agreements
- Let's always sign a contract if the agreement is above $200 or more than 2 hours of work from one side
- Let's not rely on a verbal agreement at all as people can be in an emotional state or misremember the information

Online or in-person meetings
- It's ok to be late to the meetings with each other a little bit sometimes (e.g. 5-10 minutes depending on the meeting length and who called it)
- If we are late to a meeting with each other even for 1 minute,
  let's apologize or otherwise take the responsibility for that without giving what we think are the reasons for being late as those can sound as "excuses"

Online messaging
- We will not send voice messages without explicit approval of another person in advance
- We will not delete already sent messages, if there is a typo, let's just clarify the mistake typo in the following messages
